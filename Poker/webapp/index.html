BOT STRATEGY

Algorithm: Monte Carlo Hand Evaluation

How the Bot Thinks

1. Hand Strength

Simulates thousands of random outcomes to estimate win probability

2. Pot Odds

Compares bet size to potential winnings to find +EV plays

3. Position

Plays tighter out of position, more aggressively in position

4. Bluffing

Occasionally bluffs based on board texture and betting patterns

Unlike Blackjack or Slots, poker involves imperfect information and opponent modeling. The bot uses Monte Carlo simulation to estimate its winning chances by dealing out random remaining cards thousands of times. This is similar to how professional poker software analyzes hands.
