  • You can Check or Bet (1 chip)
  • Opponent can Fold or Call

CFR learns by simulating millions of hands against itself. It tracks "regret" for each decision—how much better an alternative action would have been. Over time, it converges to a Nash Equilibrium: a strategy that cannot be exploited. Notice how the bot learns to bluff with Jack and value bet with King.
